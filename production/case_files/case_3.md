## 8. MODALITY SPLIT — THE UNEXPLAINED ELEMENT `[RECONSTRUCTED - ASSESSMENT]`

| Victim | Modality | Offender behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| V-1 (father) | Stabbed, sashimi knife, blade broke | **Improvised weapon, failed** |
| V-2 (mother) | Stabbed, household santoku | **Deliberate weapon substitution** |
| V-3 (daughter, 8) | Stabbed, household santoku | Continued |
| V-4 (son, 6) | **Strangled** | **Method changed entirely** |

Three questions follow, and the file answers none of them:

1. **Why was a different tool carried in?** A sashimi knife is not a weapon one brings to
   a house attack. Its presence suggests the offender expected a *different* context —
   or brought a knife intended for a fight.
2. **Why substitute the household's knife?** Possession of the broken blade was
   apparently of more concern to the offender than continuity of method.
3. **Why strangle V-4?** A six-year-old, after three stabbing deaths, with a working
   blade in hand.

None of these require a motive to be interesting. They require only an offender who was
making decisions.

## 10. WHY NO NAME APPEARS IN THIS FILE

This section is deliberately structured. Read it before editing.

1. **The evidence supports no identity.** Blood group, mtDNA lineage and Y-chromosome
   lineage describe a population, not a person.
2. **The offender has never been identified** by any agency, and the case is formally open.
3. **This case has a documented history of private individuals being named online without
   any basis**, repeatedly and over many years, causing demonstrable harm to uninvolved
   people.
4. **A reconstruction product has no standing to make an accusation.** A dramatized
   document is the *worst* possible place to introduce a name.

**Publishing rule for this asset:** the offender is described by conduct and forensic
marker only. No name. No photograph. No "theories" section naming individuals.
