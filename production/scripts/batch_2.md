## VOICEOVER SCRIPT (clean — feeds TTS directly)

He killed all six. // Then he stayed for days, ate their food, and fed their animals.

On the last night of March nineteen twenty-two, six people were murdered at an isolated
Bavarian farm.

The weapon was a mattock. // Four bodies were stacked in a barn. Two were in their beds.

The seven-year-old girl survived for hours, pulling her own hair out, while her family
died around her.

Then the killer stayed. He ate their meals and slept in the house.

A calendar on the wall was open to the first of April. The murder date was torn out.

The farm was demolished a year later, and the fingerprints with it.

And the dog that barked at every visitor had never once barked at one man.

The full unredacted case file is below. It names him.

## HOOK MECHANICS (first 3 seconds)

**Spoken:** *"He killed all six. Then he stayed for days, ate their food, and fed their
animals."*

- **Two-part reversal** — the first clause satisfies the expected horror beat, the
  second immediately undercuts it. The viewer's model of the scene breaks, which is
  what drives the rewatch.
- **Past-tense calm** — no present-tense dramatisation. Distance makes it worse.
- **Three concrete verbs** — ate, fed, slept. Mundane verbs against monstrous context.

## CTA MECHANICS (final 5 seconds)

- **"It names him"** — two words of pure promise. No throat-clearing, no adjectives.
- Deliberately withholds the name. The pack sells the accusation-free evidence; the
  free video sells the question.
