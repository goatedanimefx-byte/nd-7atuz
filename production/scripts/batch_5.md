> **FACT NOTE.** The previous draft of this script was **badly wrong** and has been
> discarded and rewritten. It claimed the ship was found with two men aboard and a body
> in the forecastle. The *Mary Celeste* was found **completely deserted** — nobody was
> aboard at all, no body was ever recovered, and no survivor ever gave an account. The
> "two survivors" element belonged to a different vessel. Corrected 2026-10-03 against
> Wikipedia, Britannica, Smithsonian Magazine, Royal Museums Greenwich and History.
> The corrected version is a better hook than the wrong one.

## VOICEOVER SCRIPT (clean — feeds TTS directly)

Ten people sailed into the Atlantic. // Nine days later the ship was found drifting,
alone, with nobody on deck.

December, eighteen seventy-two. An American brigantine left New York for Genoa with six
months of food aboard.

The cargo was intact. The ship was seaworthy. Every person aboard was gone.

The lifeboat was missing, and it had been stowed over the main hatch.

Both cargo hatches had been lifted off and left on deck. One pump lay in pieces beside a
sounding rod.

Three feet of water sat in the hold, far too little to sink her. The captain's papers and
instruments were gone.

No body was ever found. No wreckage of the boat. No survivor, and no message.

The last entry in her log was nine days earlier, near an island in the Azores.

## HOOK MECHANICS (first 3 seconds)

**Spoken:** *"Ten people sailed into the Atlantic. Nine days later the ship was found
drifting, alone, with nobody on deck."*

- **Two-beat reversal** — the hook's second clause cancels the first. The most reliable
  retention pattern in the batch.
- **Specific count** — "ten" makes the absence measurable; the brain immediately tries
  to subtract and finds no explanation.
- **No person appears** in the entire first sentence. Absence as the subject.

## CTA MECHANICS (final 5 seconds)

- **"Every document that survives is in it"** — a completeness promise. Positions the
  pack as the archive, not a retelling.
- Ends unresolved on purpose. The viewer is left holding the log entry, which is the
  thing that recurs in comments.
