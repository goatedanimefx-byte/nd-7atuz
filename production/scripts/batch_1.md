## VOICEOVER SCRIPT (clean — feeds TTS directly)

> Read this at a slow, level, documentary pace. No shouting. The horror is in the
> information, not the delivery. Pauses marked `//` are intentional silence.

Nine hikers walked into the snow. // Their tents were found torn open from the inside.

In February nineteen fifty-nine, Soviet students set out to cross a pass in the Urals.
By morning, all nine were dead.

Investigators found the tents ripped open from the inside. // One body lay far higher up
the slope, naked, facing a tree, jaw broken.

The official ruling: hypothermia. A slab of snow. An infrasound storm that made people
run until they dropped.

But that explanation needs one photo to work. // And the photo does not hold up.

One survivor was photographed that day. But a second camera rolled straight past his
face. // He was never in frame.

So what left those tents?

The full unredacted case file is in the link below. It's longer than this. And it's worse.
Turn the lights on.

## HOOK MECHANICS (first 3 seconds)

**Spoken:** *"Nine hikers walked into the snow. Their tents were found torn open from
the inside."*

- **Number specificity** — "nine" is concrete; viewers self-insert into a group.
- **Contradiction** — people went in, something came out, or got out.
- **Passive-voice ambush** — "were found" delays the subject. The brain completes it wrong.
- **Second sentence is the real hook.** Front-load it. Never cut it for time.

## CTA MECHANICS (final 5 seconds)

Three-step CTA: **value promise → friction warning → implied dare.**
1. *"the full unredacted case file"* — names the asset.
2. *"longer than this. and it's worse"* — reframes the free video as the trailer.
3. *"you'll want the lights on"* — lifestyle cue, raises stakes without a scare gimmick.
