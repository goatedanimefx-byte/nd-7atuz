## VOICEOVER SCRIPT (clean — feeds TTS directly)

He murdered all four of them. // Then he stayed for hours and ate their ice cream.

On New Year's Eve two thousand, a father, a mother, and two small children were killed
in a Tokyo suburb.

The father was stabbed first with a sashimi knife. The blade snapped inside his skull,
so the killer took a kitchen knife from the house and finished the rest.

Except the six-year-old boy. He was strangled. Different method.

And then he stayed. He ate four containers of ice cream, drank their tea, and used their
toilet without flushing.

He left his own blood behind, his clothes in the hallway, and three powders on his shoes.

Five million fingerprints. One point three million DNA tests. Two hundred and eighty
thousand officers. Twenty-five years.

No match.

The full unredacted case file is below. It is four hundred pages. Almost nobody has read
it.

## HOOK MECHANICS (first 3 seconds)

**Spoken:** *"He murdered all four of them. Then he stayed for hours and ate their
ice cream."*

- **Absurd juxtaposition** — the tonal collision between the two clauses *is* the hook.
  The brain cannot file it, so it rewatches.
- **Specific number** — "four" sets up the escalation when the six-year-old differs.
- Deliberately non-graphic: the horror is behavioural, not descriptive, which keeps it
  inside ad-safe review.

## CTA MECHANICS (final 5 seconds)

- **"Almost nobody has read it"** — indicts the audience, which reliably drives comments
  defending either their own reading or the video's.
- Frames the pack as *primary source material*, not a summary — the highest-value
  framing for this genre.
